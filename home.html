📺 The broadcast

Every round goes final on a big-screen reveal. Movement arrows, the pond, the current leader. Saturday night ends with a coronation, a chain on the champion’s portrait and his name on the wall.

🏇 Fairwaze Downs

Eight horses, real odds, bet as many as you like. Set a post time and it runs itself, then plays a 14-second race every phone watches together.

💳 Credit card roulette

Dinner’s over, everybody puts a card in, the wheel picks who pays. Splits the bill, posts the verdict to the feed.

🎡 Wheel of Misfortune

Last place spins for a punishment the group wrote. Scramble losers go on the wheel together. One spin, one fall guy, one sentence.

💰 Side bets that settle themselves

Me vs him on any round, team vs team, strokes on or off. Rounds go final, bets pay, Settle Up nets it all into one Venmo tap.

🃏 The Duel & ⛳ The Imaginary Open

High card for cash when you’re bored at the bar. Or a nine-hole skins match you play from your phone all week, with presses, concessions and a replay of every shot.

🗣️ The feed

Photos, trash talk, ten reactions, a 🎤 button that writes the line for you and shouts FORE. Line of the day makes the recap.

💸 The Bystander Tax

Every day before the trip with no bet, post or duel costs a guy $2.50, capped at $25. The pot goes to the commissioner, to do with as he sees fit. Nobody goes quiet in September.

🍺 Gulp Fiction

Beer count per golfer per round. It ends up in the awards. Thirstiest Golfer is a real trophy.

📰 Recaps & highlights

A recap posts after every day with the money, shot of the day and a highlight reel built from what the group posted. A Sunday-morning wrap-up closes it out.

🔔 It buzzes everyone

Bets, challenges, your turn, post time in an hour, round final, recap ready. Nobody has to open the app to know something happened.

🖼️ Made for your group

Your names, handicaps, portraits drawn as comic art, bios, a memorial for the guy who bailed, past champions, your own anthem.

📅 Any format

Three-day trip or one Saturday. Scrambles, shambles, best ball, stroke play, skins. Best-N-of-M counting, drop the worst, all set up once.
